Help & FAQ

Last updated 6 November 2026

Quick answers for people joining plans and people hosting them. Can't find what you need? In the app, go to You → Feedback and ask us. A real person replies, right there in the app.

Getting started

What is ghulmil?

ghulmil helps you find people nearby to do things with: a game of football, a chess evening, a run, a trek, a coffee meet-up. Anyone can post a plan, and you ask to join the ones you like.

Who can use ghulmil?

Anyone aged 18 or over. Use your real name and a current photo. See our Community Guidelines.

Can I see the app tour again?

Yes. Go to Settings → App tour.

How do I switch on dark mode?

Go to Settings → Appearance. Use the sun and moon toggle, or turn on Match phone setting to follow your phone's light or dark mode.

Joining plans

How do I find plans near me?

The Home and Explore tabs show what's happening around you. In Explore you can filter by activity, day and distance, or swipe through plans one by one in Quick match.

How do I join a plan?

Open the plan and ask to join. You can add a short message for the host. The host approves or declines each request, and you get a notification either way. Once you're in, you can read and post in the plan's feed.

What does the waitlist mean?

When a plan is full, new requests go on the waitlist. If someone leaves, the next person on the waitlist moves in automatically and is notified.

My plans changed. How do I drop out?

Open the plan, tap the (i) button and choose Leave this activity. Please leave as early as you can, so someone on the waitlist can take your spot. Leaving before the start never counts against you.

Where are the plans I've joined or saved?

In the Plans tab. The You tab also has Saved activities and Plans & history.

Check-in and your haazri score

How do I check in when I arrive?

Check-in opens 30 minutes before the start. Open the plan and either scan the QR code on the host's phone, type the host's 6-character code, or check in by location if you're within about 300 metres of the venue.

What is the haazri score?

It's your show-up rate: how many of the plans you were marked for you actually came to. Hosts see it when you ask to join, so turning up helps you get approved.

What happens if I'm marked as a no-show?

Each no-show is a strike. After the second strike we send you a warning. Three strikes within 90 days, from at least two different hosts, pause joining for 30 days. After a pause, you start again with a clean slate.

If you checked yourself in, a host can't mark you as a no-show. Hosts can also correct a mistake for up to 48 hours after the plan ends. If you think a strike is wrong, email support@ghulmil.app.

Hosting

How do I host a plan?

Tap +, pick the activity, then add the time, place, number of spots and any details: the vibe, skill level and cost per person. Your plan goes live straight away for people nearby.

How do I approve people?

You get a notification for every join request. Open it, or go to You → Join requests, to approve or decline. You can see each person's profile, verification tick and haazri score first.

Can I edit or cancel my plan?

You can edit a plan up until 2 hours before it starts. Everyone who is going or waiting is told what changed. Once someone has joined or asked to join, the cost and the kind of activity are fixed. If you need to cancel, do it as early as you can. Everyone is notified automatically.

How does check-in work for hosts?

When check-in opens, show the QR code or read out the code from your plan. After the plan, mark who came in roll call, within 48 hours of the end. It keeps everyone's haazri score honest.

Can I charge for a plan?

Only to cover genuine costs, like a court booking or equipment, and only the amount shown on the plan.

What is a toli?

A toli is a group that runs plans week after week, like a Sunday football crew or a monthly book club. Join a toli to hear about every plan it posts. Joining each plan still goes through the host's approval. Anyone with the blue tick can start a toli, up to 3 each.

Verification and safety

How do I get the blue tick?

Take a live profile selfie with the front camera, and confirm your email with a one-time code. Both steps are in the You tab under Get verified. Hosts approve verified people faster.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap ⋯ on a profile, plan or post, or press and hold a comment, then choose Report or Block. Reports are confidential, and we review every one within 24 hours. Manage blocked people in Settings → Blocked users.

How do I stay safe when meeting people?
  • Meet in public places, especially the first time.
  • Tell a friend or family member where you're going.
  • Check the blue tick and haazri score before you go.
  • You can leave any plan at any time.
  • If you're in immediate danger, call 112 (in India) first, then report it to us.

Your account

How do I send feedback or report a bug?

Go to You → Feedback. Pick a kind (suggestion, bug, question, something you love, or other) and write your message. When we reply, you'll get a notification and see the reply under your message.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photos, posts and comments, and can't be undone. See Delete your account for details.

How is my information used?

See our Privacy Policy.
