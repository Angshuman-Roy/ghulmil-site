How it works

From “koi plan hai?” to showing up.

  1. 01

    Sign in, in a tap.

    Continue with Google or use your email. ghulmil is for adults 18+, and a quick selfie gets you the blue tick.

  2. 02

    Pick a kind, or start from scratch.

    Sports, mind & learning, creative, music, outdoor, gaming: 42 activities in all. Post a plan in a minute and people nearby see it fast.

  3. 03

    Tap Chalo! and you're on the list.

    When a host lets you in, the plan lands in your week. Going, hosting, saved and past, all in one place.

  4. 04

    Save it for later.

    Not today? Swipe up in Quick match, or tap the bookmark on any plan, and park it in Saved.
