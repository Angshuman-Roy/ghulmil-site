How it works

From “koi plan hai?” to showing up.

  1. 🏏

    Find a plan nearby

    Cricket, treks, board games, chai walks: see what's happening around you.

  2. ☕

    Ask to join

    The host sees who you are and lets you in. You'll get a ping when you're in.

  3. ♟️

    Show up and meet your people

    Check in when you get there, then keep the chat going in the plan's feed.

  4. 📚

    Or host your own

    Pick the time and place, set how many can come, and choose who joins.
